# Competitor Feature Analysis — StayFree · AppBlock · YPT

A consolidated, deduplicated feature and use-case inventory of three digital-wellbeing / productivity apps: **StayFree** (cross-device screen-time tracker & usage control), **AppBlock** (app & website blocker), and **YPT (YeolPumTa)** (study-focused time tracker). Shared features are listed **once** with the apps that provide them, so nothing is repeated.

Sources: each app's official site, user guide, and Google Play / Microsoft Store listing. Research date: 2026-08-20.

## Detailed features

### Tracking & analytics

- **F01 — Per-app / per-site screen-time tracking.** StayFree and AppBlock track time across apps and websites on phone and desktop; YPT tracks study time per subject. (All)
- **F02 — Usage analytics & visualizations.** Easy-to-read graphs and cross-device stats (StayFree); statistics showing time spent per app/site and screen-time drop (AppBlock); day/week/month study visualization where color deepens the more you study (YPT). (All)
- **F15 — Pickup / unlock counter.** Counts how often the phone is picked up or unlocked per day. (StayFree)
- **F28 — Usage-data exclusions.** Ignore work-related or non-distracting websites from stats; optionally exclude tabs playing audio/video in the background. (StayFree)

### Blocking & limits

- **F03 — Block apps & websites.** One-tap or configured blocks on distracting apps, sites, URLs, and keywords. (StayFree, AppBlock, YPT focus sessions)
- **F04 — In-app content filtering.** Hide the addictive parts of social media — Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat Stories — without blocking the entire app. (StayFree in-app blocking, AppBlock on Android)
- **F05 — Daily / session usage limits.** Cap total usage per app, site, or category per day, or set a per-session limit ("Limits On The Go"). (StayFree, AppBlock)
- **F06 — Scheduled blocking.** Automate blocking at set times and days for work, study, sleep, or relaxation. (StayFree, AppBlock)
- **F07 — Focus mode / focus session.** One-tap or scheduled deep-focus mode that silences blocked apps/sites. In YPT it's a focus session that blocks distracting apps while a study timer runs. (All)
- **F08 — Strict Mode.** Makes blocks very hard to bypass: disables changing settings, disabling blocks, or uninstalling during temptation. AppBlock's signature feature (55% of customers say they can't live without it; ~63% less screen time in the first month). (AppBlock)
- **F09 — Allowlist / dumb-phone mode.** Block everything except user-chosen essentials (calls, messages, maps, banking, work tools), turning the phone into a simplified device. (AppBlock)
- **F10 — Keyword & URL-level website blocking.** Block whole sites or specific URLs/keywords. (StayFree, AppBlock)
- **F11 — Adult-content blocking.** A large database of 18+ URLs, usable for personal detox or parental control. (AppBlock)
- **F12 — Context-triggered blocks.** Profiles that activate based on time, day of week, location, or connected Wi-Fi. (AppBlock)
- **F13 — Work/study/sleep profiles.** Ready-made blocking profiles for different parts of the day. (StayFree schedules, AppBlock profiles)
- **F31 — Dumb-phone simplification mode.** Reduce the phone to essential functions during high-focus periods. (AppBlock)
- **F32 — Built-in productivity timer.** Manage time effectively with a timer alongside blocking. (AppBlock)

### Alerts, limits & routines

- **F14 — Over-use alerts & reminders.** Notify when too much time is spent in an app, prompting a digital-detox moment. (StayFree)
- **F16 — Bedtime & daily reset scheduling.** Schedule time away from the phone (bedtime reminders) and set when daily stats reset. (StayFree)

### Reports, goals & habit building

- **F19 — Daily / weekly / monthly reports.** Progress reports on usage reduction and time distribution. (StayFree, AppBlock, YPT)
- **F20 — Goal setting & habit tracking.** Set usage goals and build longer-term digital habits. (StayFree, AppBlock)
- **F33 — Digital-detox / dopamine-detox toolkits.** Guided routines to break overstimulating tech patterns, control doomscrolling, and reduce compulsive checking. (StayFree, AppBlock)

### Study-specific (YPT)

- **F21 — Subject-based study time tracking.** Start/stop timers per subject and log hours to each. (YPT)
- **F22 — To-do list & 10-minute daily planner.** Plan the day's study tasks; review daily study logs and check off to-dos. (YPT)
- **F23 — Study groups.** Create groups to see friends' status and study activity, and study together remotely. (YPT)
- **F24 — Realtime rankings.** Rank yourself within the same study category as millions of other students. (YPT)
- **F25 — Wear OS companion.** Track study time on a Galaxy Watch 4+ (requires in-app purchase): check current study time, today's total, D-Day, subjects, and group members' time via complications. (YPT)
- **F26 — Study streaks.** Community-requested daily-study streak feature for motivation. (YPT)

### Ecosystem & platform features

- **F17 — Cross-device usage sync.** Unified view of usage across phone, tablet, and desktop (StayFree manage-devices; AppBlock browser + mobile).
- **F18 — Browser extensions.** StayFree and AppBlock both ship Chrome/Edge/Brave extensions for desktop website blocking and tracking.
- **F27 — Custom new-tab dashboard.** Extension turns the new-tab page into a personalized usage-statistics dashboard. (StayFree)
- **F29 — Backup & restore.** Export/import usage data, delete history, and reset settings. (StayFree)
- **F30 — PIN protection.** Require a PIN before changing important settings. (StayFree)

## Use cases by audience

### Students
- **StayFree**: cap YouTube/social usage during exam season; schedule focus windows that match class blocks; track which subjects steal study time; use the pickup counter to spot phone-checking habits.
- **AppBlock**: block gaming/social apps during homework with Strict Mode so temptation can't be clicked away; keep study tools via Allowlist; profile per class schedule (Study profile 8am–3pm).
- **YPT**: the core study timer — log hours per subject, plan the day with the 10-minute planner and to-do list, join a study group to study "together" remotely, and use rankings to stay competitive and consistent. Often used by NEET/JEE/UPSC/board-exam aspirants tracking 10+ hours daily.

### Professionals / deep work
- **StayFree**: track work vs. personal usage across laptop and phone; block news/social during deep-work sprints; weekly report to audit where time went.
- **AppBlock**: Focus Mode + productivity timer for Pomodoro-style sprints; block distracting sites during meetings; location/Wi-Fi-triggered profiles (office = strict, home = relaxed).
- **YPT**: subject-based tracking doubles as project/task time logging; group accountability with colleagues or study buddies.

### Parents & families
- **StayFree**: monitor kids' usage across their devices; bedtime schedule to wind down; over-use alerts.
- **AppBlock**: block inappropriate/adult content via the 18+ database; set screen-time boundaries; Strict Mode keeps kids from unblocking; dumb-phone mode for "family time."
- **YPT**: less relevant — primarily a self-tracking study tool.

### Digital detox & mental health
- **StayFree**: over-use reminders start a detox moment; daily limits ease off addictive apps; cross-device stats expose the real total.
- **AppBlock**: dopamine-detox and doomscrolling control toolkits; strict blocks on infinite-scroll feeds (Reels/Shorts); reclaiming hours (users report ~3 hours/day reclaimed on average).
- **YPT**: focus sessions double as a distraction-free study environment.

### ADHD & neurodivergent support
- **AppBlock** is frequently cited by ADHD users for blocking the dopamine-inducing tactics of social platforms; Strict Mode works when willpower doesn't. StayFree's limits + reminders serve the same purpose with a softer touch.

## Differentiation summary (why each exists)

- **StayFree** — the **analytics-first** tracker: deep cross-device usage insight, reports, reminders, and in-app feed filtering. Blocking is a tool, insight is the product.
- **AppBlock** — the **enforcement-first** blocker: un-bypassable Strict Mode, allowlist/dumb-phone mode, context-aware schedules, and content-level (Reels/Shorts) blocking. Focus is the product.
- **YPT** — the **social study timer**: time tracking + to-do planning + group accountability + rankings + Wear OS. Motivation through community and competition is the product.
